Under His Cover

October 8, 2026 • Devotion

⏱ 2 Minute Read

“He who dwells in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.” — Psalm 91:1

God’s covering is more than a place of safety—it is a place of surrender. When we choose to live under God’s authority, we position ourselves to experience His guidance, protection, and peace.

Sometimes we resist authority because we want to do things our own way. But true freedom is not found in rebellion; it is found in trusting God enough to surrender our plans, preferences, and pride to Him.

Today, let’s ask ourselves: Am I fully under God’s covering, or am I trying to lead my own life?

Surrender is not weakness. It is trust.

Prayer

Lord, help me to live under Your covering today. Give me a humble and willing heart that trusts Your authority, even when I don’t understand Your ways. Remove every rebellious attitude and teach me to walk in obedience, peace, and faith. In Jesus’ name, amen.

God, I’m Still Grateful for You

September 14, 2026 • Devotion

⏱ 5 Minute Read

There are moments in life when I have to stop and remind myself that God is still good. Sometimes I may not understand what He is doing. Sometimes I may be walking through a difficult season, facing disappointment, loss, uncertainty, or simply feeling tired. But even in those moments, I can still say, “God, I’m still grateful for You.”

My gratitude cannot be based only on what God does for me. My gratitude has to be rooted in who God is.

I am grateful because He has never stopped being God. He has carried me through things I didn't think I could get through. He has given me strength when I felt weak, peace when my mind was troubled, and hope when I couldn't see what was ahead.

Psalm 100:4–5 reminds me:

“Enter into His gates with thanksgiving,
And into His courts with praise.
Be thankful to Him, and bless His name.
For the LORD is good;
His mercy is everlasting,
And His truth endures to all generations.”
Psalm 100:4–5, NKJV

Notice that the Psalm doesn't say to praise God only when everything is going well. We are called to enter with thanksgiving and praise because the Lord is good.

There are times when my prayer may simply be, “Lord, I don't understand this, but I'm still grateful for You.” I may not have every answer. I may not know what tomorrow holds. But I know the One who holds tomorrow.

First Thessalonians 5:18 says:

“In everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.”
1 Thessalonians 5:18, NKJV

Giving thanks in everything doesn't mean that everything that happens to me is good. It means that even in difficult circumstances, I can find something to thank God for.

I can thank Him for another morning.

I can thank Him for another opportunity to worship.

I can thank Him for the people He has placed in my life.

I can thank Him for the strength to keep moving forward.

I can thank Him for His grace and mercy.

And above all, I can thank Him simply because He is still with me.

Sometimes a test becomes a testimony. Sometimes a mess becomes a message. Sometimes the very thing that tried to break me becomes the place where I discover just how faithful God really is.

So today, regardless of what I am facing, I choose gratitude.

God, I may not understand everything. I may not have everything I want. I may still be walking through some things. But God, I'm still grateful for You.

Because You are faithful.

You are merciful.

You are loving.

You are my refuge.

You are my strength.

And You are still God.

My circumstances may change, but God's goodness does not change.

Thought-Provoking Question

If God didn't change my circumstances right away, could I still lift my hands and sincerely say, “God, I'm still grateful for You”—and what would I thank Him for today?

Prayer

Lord, thank You for being faithful even when I don't understand what You are doing. Help me not to measure Your goodness by my circumstances. Teach me to have a grateful heart in every season. When life is difficult, remind me of how far You have already brought me. When I am blessed, keep me humble and thankful. And when I don't have the answers, help me trust the One who does. God, I'm still grateful for You. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Write the Vision

September 7, 2026 • Devotion

⏱ 5 Minute Read

God is a God of purpose, and He is not careless with the plans He places in our hearts. There are moments when He gives us a glimpse of what He intends to do before He reveals every detail. In those moments, we must learn to trust His direction even when we cannot yet see the entire path.

The Lord instructed the prophet Habakkuk, “Then the LORD answered me and said: ‘Write the vision And make it plain on tablets, That he may run who reads it’” (Habakkuk 2:2, NKJV).

God told Habakkuk to write the vision and make it plain. This teaches us that what God reveals should not be treated casually. His direction deserves to be remembered, understood, and acted upon. Writing the vision creates a record of what He has placed before us and helps us remain focused when circumstances attempt to distract us.

Sometimes God gives us a vision before He gives us the details. We may see where He is leading without knowing exactly how we will get there. That is where faith becomes necessary. The vision may look bigger than our present circumstances, but God does not require us to understand everything before we obey Him.

Writing the vision also requires clarity. When God gives you a purpose, be intentional about it. Pray over it. Write it down. Seek His wisdom. Let His Word shape your plans, and keep your heart sensitive to His leading. A vision that is written can be revisited, prayed over, and used as a reminder of what God has placed in your heart.

The final part of the verse says, “that he may run who reads it.” God’s vision is not given merely to inspire us. It is given to move us. There comes a time when we must stop hesitating and start walking in obedience.

Faith is not simply believing that God can do something. Faith also means being willing to follow Him when He calls us forward. We may not know every step, but we can take the step He has placed before us.

You may not have everything figured out today. You may even be waiting for God to reveal the next step. But if He has given you a promise, hold on to it. If He has given you a direction, follow it. If He has placed a vision in your heart, do not allow fear, delay, or discouragement to make you abandon it.

What God has spoken requires faith, patience, and action. The waiting season does not mean the vision has been forgotten. Sometimes God is preparing us for what He has already placed before us.

So write the vision. Make it plain. Pray over it. Trust God's timing, and be willing to run when He says it is time to move.

God has a purpose for your life, and when you walk in His direction, your obedience can become part of the testimony He is writing through you.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for the vision and purpose You have placed before me. Help me to hear Your voice clearly, write down what You reveal, and make it plain in my heart. Give me wisdom to understand Your direction and courage to act upon it. When the journey becomes difficult or the fulfillment seems delayed, help me remain faithful and trust Your timing. Let my life run according to Your purpose and bring glory to Your name. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

Reflection

What has God placed on your heart that you need to write down, pray over, and begin pursuing in faith?

Remember: Write it. Make it plain. Trust God. Run with the vision.

He Gave Me a Reason to Dance

August 31, 2026 • Devotion

⏱ 5 Minute Read

There are seasons when life feels heavy. We carry disappointments, unanswered prayers, heartbreak, loss, and moments when we wonder if our joy will ever return. Sometimes, the weight of yesterday makes it difficult to imagine celebrating tomorrow. Yet God has a way of changing our story.

The psalmist declares, “You have turned my mourning into dancing; You have put off my sackcloth and clothed me with gladness, To the end that my glory may sing praise to You and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks to You forever” (Psalm 30:11–12, NKJV).

Notice that it was God who made the transformation. The psalmist did not dance because everything had always been easy. He danced because God had brought him through. His praise was born from experiencing the faithfulness of God in the middle of difficult circumstances.

There is something powerful about realizing that God does not waste our painful seasons. The tears we cried can become testimonies. The battles we thought would break us can become reminders of His strength. The places where we experienced disappointment can become places where we discover His faithfulness.

God gives us a reason to dance, not because life is perfect, but because He is faithful.

When He restores our hope, He gives us a reason to dance. When He forgives our sins, He gives us a reason to dance. When He carries us through the valley, He gives us a reason to dance. When He answers our prayers, or gives us the grace to keep trusting while we wait, He gives us a reason to dance.

Our dance is an expression of gratitude.

David understood this. His praise was not dependent on his circumstances. He had learned that God's presence was greater than his problems. His worship became a declaration: I may have cried, but God did not abandon me. I may have struggled, but God carried me. I may have fallen, but God lifted me.

Perhaps today you are still in a season of mourning. If so, do not mistake your current chapter for the end of your story. God is still writing. Your tears do not mean God has forgotten you. Your waiting does not mean God is absent. Your struggle does not mean God has stopped working.

There will come a time when you look back and realize that the same God who sustained you through the night was preparing you for a new morning.

So lift your head. Open your heart. Remember what God has already done.

You may not have everything you prayed for yet, but you still have a faithful God. Sometimes, that realization is enough to make your heart sing again.

He gave you a reason to dance.

Not because there were never tears, but because His grace was greater than the tears. Not because there were never battles, but because He was with you in every battle. Not because the road was always easy, but because He never stopped walking beside you.

Today, let your praise rise from your testimony. Let your worship declare that God has been good, God is good, and God will continue to be good.

The mourning did not have the final word. God did.

And when God turns mourning into dancing, do not be afraid to dance.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for being with me through every season of my life. Thank You for carrying me when I was weak, comforting me when I was hurting, and giving me hope when I could not see a way forward. Turn my mourning into dancing and my sorrow into praise. Help me remember Your faithfulness and trust You with the chapters I do not yet understand. May my life become a testimony of Your grace, and may my worship always reflect how good You have been to me. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Reflection

What has God brought you through that gives you a reason to praise Him today?

My Strength Is from the Lord

August 24, 2026 • Devotion

⏱ 4 Minute Read

Life can bring moments when we feel weak, overwhelmed, or uncertain about what lies ahead. Yet our strength does not have to come from our own ability. The Lord is our source, our helper, and our refuge.

The psalmist declares, “The LORD is my strength and my shield; My heart trusted in Him, and I am helped” (Psalm 28:7, NKJV). When we place our trust in God, He gives us the strength to face what we cannot handle on our own.

Isaiah 40:31 reminds us, “But those who wait on the LORD Shall renew their strength; They shall mount up with wings like eagles, They shall run and not be weary, They shall walk and not faint.” Waiting on God is not weakness. It is an expression of faith. He renews us when our strength is exhausted.

Paul also reminds us in Philippians 4:13, “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” Our confidence is not in ourselves but in Christ, who equips us for every season.

Whatever you are facing today, remember: your strength comes from the Lord. You may feel weak, but God is faithful. You may not know what tomorrow holds, but He holds tomorrow. Trust Him, wait on Him, and draw your strength from Him.

Prayer

Lord, thank You for being my strength when I am weak and my help when I am overwhelmed. Teach me to trust You completely and to depend on Your power rather than my own. Renew my strength, increase my faith, and help me walk confidently in Your presence. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
